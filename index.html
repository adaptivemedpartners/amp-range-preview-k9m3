FAQ · AI-cite ready

Physician recruiting questions AMP answers plainly

Same core set as our JSON-LD FAQ — mountain voice, public proof only, no invented towns.

What is the difference between retained and contingent physician search?

Retained search funds a dedicated guide and workplan up front so hospitals wait at the peak with a clear brief while AMP prepares candidates. Contingent search typically pays only on placement and often becomes a multi-firm race to resumes. AMP emphasizes retained craft for confidential, strategic seats; contingent (MPC) is the contrast model for wilderness exploration without a guide.

How do physicians start with Adaptive Medical Partners?

Enter the physician door (#physician): choose specialty → region → open a practice-first job tease → talk with a guide (call, text, email, or chat). Public pages stay tease-level; full packages stay on a confidential conversation. You can also run AMP Score (#amp-score) or browse Education (#education).

How do hospitals and medical groups start with AMP?

Enter the hiring / client door (#client): facility type → specialty → review a retained tease → request a meeting. AMP educates both sides so the organization waits with a brief worth defending — not a dossier dump on the open web.

What does AMP’s 87% retention at 3 years mean?

It is the share of AMP placements still in role (or system) at the three-year mark. Placement is reaching the peak; retention is staying on the mountain. It is public proof of durable fit — not a personal guarantee for every search, and not the same as time-to-fill.

What does 1.7 average interviews per placement mean?

It is AMP’s reported mean number of interviews per successful placement. The point is summit-meeting efficiency: prepare hikers on the trail so committees do not burn cycles on candidates who were never summit-ready. It is a process KPI from About language, not a promise that every search has a two-person slate.

Does AMP focus on rural, FQHC, and critical access recruiting?

Yes. AMP cites partnerships with 700+ rural hospitals, FQHCs, and critical access facilities, alongside the retention and interview-efficiency proofs. Public education stays facility-agnostic on purpose — no invented town or hospital names.

What is AMP Score?

AMP Score is a fit explainer — lifestyle, practice design, compensation clarity, culture signals, and summit readiness — not a public hospital ranking or a credit score for doctors. Try the interactive score at #amp-score; scores inform guide conversations, not hard public verdicts.

How does AMP protect confidentiality?

Public job pages are trail signs: practice-first headlines, limited bullets, lifestyle heroes when useful. Named facility deep-dives, non-public financials, and full stacks stay gated for confidential calls and summit meetings. Dual applying around your guide creates crossed wires — let AMP coordinate when representing the search.

Education before opportunity

Coming out of training?

Your first job is a summit worth choosing carefully. AMP is the guide: you’re searching for the peak, while the right client is waiting at the peak. Start with the shortfalls before you click into roles.

As reported by the AMA: nearly 60% leave their first post-training job within three years, and more than 25% consider leaving within year one. Treat that as a prompt to ask better questions—not as a prediction about your path.
01

Shortfalls

Notice what the brochure leaves out: the actual week, call architecture, team support, and what changes after the guarantee.

02

Place first

Region matters, but place is more than a label. Ask where you will practice, who you will become there, and what your partner needs too.

03

Contracts

Build contract literacy before you sign: compensation mechanics, schedule language, restrictive covenants, termination, and who answers questions.

04

Second climb

If the first ridge is wrong, a second climb is not failure. Learn how to audit the fit before you carry a new pack.
