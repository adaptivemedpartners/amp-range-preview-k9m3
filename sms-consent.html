Adaptive Medical Partners: Internal SMS Lead Alerts

Program: Adaptive Medical Partners, Inc. sends automated text alerts from toll-free number +1 (833) 709-7353 only to its own employees (recruiters, business development staff, and management). When a physician candidate or hospital client submits an inquiry on our website, the assigned employee gets a text so they can follow up quickly. We never text customers or the public, and we don't send marketing.

How employees opt in (verbal consent)

Before an employee's mobile number is added to the alert list, an Adaptive Medical Partners manager reads the script below to the employee. The number is added only if the employee answers "Yes." The manager records the employee's name, the mobile number, the date and time of consent, and the manager's name.

"Adaptive Medical Partners will send you automated text alerts about new website leads and hospital client inquiries at the mobile number you provide. These alerts are for your work only. Consent is not a condition of employment. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help. Do you agree to receive these texts?"

Sample message

Adaptive Medical Partners: New client meeting request for Randy - Jane Smith, 555-555-0100, Texas, OB/GYN. Details in email. Reply STOP to opt out.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to any alert to stop all messages right away. Reply HELP for help, or contact mfreeman@adaptivemedicalpartners.com. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply.

Privacy

We use employee mobile numbers only to send these internal alerts. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.
