Text Messaging for Candidates – Adaptive Medical Partners, Inc.

Who we are: Adaptive Medical Partners, Inc. (AMP) is a healthcare staffing and physician recruiting firm based in Irving, Texas. Our recruiters help physicians and other clinicians find the right positions with hospitals and healthcare organizations.

What texts you'll get: AMP recruiters may text you about healthcare job opportunities that match your background, interviews (confirmations and reminders), scheduling calls and interviews, offers and onboarding, and follow-ups on your application – only if you've agreed. Messages are one-to-one conversations with your assigned recruiter, and you can reply.

How you opt in

You opt in by checking the SMS consent box when applying on our website or through a job board application, or by giving verbal consent to your AMP recruiter by phone. We record the date, time, and method of your consent before any text is sent.

"I agree to receive recruiting text messages from Adaptive Medical Partners about job opportunities, interviews and my application. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of employment."

Consent is not a condition of employment, placement, or any service. You can work with AMP without agreeing to texts.

Frequency, cost, opt-out and help

  • Frequency: message frequency varies with your job search.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time. You'll get one confirmation and no further texts. Reply START to resubscribe.
  • Help: reply HELP, call 888-885-5009, or visit adaptivemedicalpartners.com.

Sample message

Hi Jane, this is Amy with Adaptive Medical Partners. We have an OB/GYN opening in Austin, TX that fits your background. Open to a quick call this week? Reply STOP to opt out.

Privacy

We do not sell or share your mobile number or text opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. See our Privacy Policy.
