FAQ

Physician recruiting questions AMP answers plainly

Plain voice, public proof only, no invented towns.

What is the difference between dedicated and contingent physician search?

Dedicated investment search funds a dedicated guide and workplan up front so hospitals wait with a clear brief while AMP prepares candidates. Contingent search typically pays only on placement and often becomes a multi-firm race to resumes. AMP emphasizes dedicated search craft for confidential, strategic seats; MPC opens a tailored search with a guide when posted roles don’t fit.

How do physicians start with Adaptive Medical Partners?

Enter the physician door (#physician): choose specialty → region → open a practice-first job preview → talk with a guide (call, text, email, or chat). Public pages stay preview-level; full packages stay on a confidential conversation. You can also run AMP Score (#amp-score) or browse Why AMP (#education).

How do hospitals and medical groups start with AMP?

Enter the hiring / client door (#client): facility type → specialty → review a hiring preview → request a meeting. AMP educates both sides so the organization waits with a brief worth defending — full details stay on a confidential call.

What does AMP’s 87% retention at 3 years mean?

It is the share of AMP placements still in role (or system) at the three-year mark. Placement is the hire; retention is staying in role. It is public proof of durable fit — not a personal guarantee for every search, and not the same as time-to-fill.

What does 1.7 average interviews per placement mean?

It is AMP’s reported mean number of interviews per successful placement. The point is interview efficiency: prepare candidates so committees do not burn cycles on people who were never interview-ready. It is a process KPI from About language, not a promise that every search has a two-person slate.

Does AMP focus on rural, FQHC, and critical access recruiting?

Yes. AMP cites partnerships with 700+ rural hospitals, FQHCs, and critical access facilities, alongside the retention and interview-efficiency proofs. Public education stays facility-agnostic — no invented town or hospital names.

What is AMP Score?

AMP Score is a fit explainer — lifestyle, practice design, compensation clarity, culture signals, and interview readiness — not a public hospital ranking or a credit score for doctors. Try the interactive score at #amp-score; scores inform guide conversations, not hard public verdicts.

How does AMP protect confidentiality?

Public job pages are previews: practice-first headlines, limited bullets, lifestyle heroes when useful. Named facility deep-dives, non-public financials, and full stacks stay gated for confidential calls and hiring meetings. Dual applying around your guide creates crossed wires — let AMP coordinate when representing the search.

Citeable answers for hiring organizations and physician guides — why Market Intelligence sits ahead of a search, and what AMP’s public retention proof means in plain language.

What is Market Intelligence?

Market Intelligence is AMP’s public specialty × state market read — supply signals, desirability/place-draw framing, and planning lenses for physicians and healthcare organizations. It is a free-depth teaser of how AMP reads markets before a search; paid geo plans and deeper firm tools stay gated. Public data only — AMP market bands when set (else pending); no MGMA percentiles, no named towns or hospitals.

Why use Market Intelligence before a search?

Market depth first means you see competition, draw, and specialty × geography context before you burn interviews or post a vague brief. Candidates use it to weigh place and practice; hiring orgs use it to set a brief worth defending. AMP’s wedge is retention craft plus Market Intelligence — knowing the market before the search, not after a resume race.
